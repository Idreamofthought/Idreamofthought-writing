The Night Before

’Twas the night before the night before Christmas,
And all through the house,
Everyone was moving,
Even the mouse.

Brush the cobwebs from the rafters,
And dust off with care
The fluff from the shelves,
And the elf that lives there.

Deck the halls with decorations,
And cook up a feast.
Fill the fridge with goodies,
And a drink for the priest.

Sit down for a tipple,
And munch a mince pie.
Watch the telly on the sofa,
And think with a sigh,

That soon it’s all over,
And soon starts again,
The magic of Christmas,
And peace to all men.

Richard M. Jenkins
