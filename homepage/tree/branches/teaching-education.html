Education — Learning Unbound

A humanistic adventure. Learning with purpose, curiosity and freedom.

By Richard M. Jenkins · 25 November 2026

Learning belongs to life

Education is not simply the transmission of information or preparation for an examination. It is an opportunity to discover the world, develop ourselves and communicate with others. Learning Unbound is the educational branch of I Dream of Thought, founded on the belief that every learner is unique, deserves to feel valued and has something valuable to contribute.

Three founding pillars

Connection

Feeling safe, seen and valued. Learning begins with trust, respect and the freedom to make mistakes without humiliation.

Construction

Building knowledge, purpose and motivation. Learners connect new ideas with their experiences, create, investigate and develop understanding through meaningful activity.

Conversation

Building real curiosity rather than blind acceptance. We ask questions, listen, exchange perspectives, challenge ideas respectfully and discover together.

The LIFE Curriculum

Life is our curriculum. A robin in a garden can lead to language, ecology, observation and conversation. A failed spaghetti tower can lead to engineering, reflection and discovery. Subjects need not be isolated from one another. We offer clear guidance and purposeful practice when useful, alongside freedom to explore.

Freedom demands increased rigour from the designer: accessible learning, accurate explanations, thoughtful progression, retrieval and spaced practice, meaningful feedback and opportunities to transfer knowledge into new situations. Our approach is humanistic in philosophy, scientific in methodology and creative in delivery. Its effectiveness must be tested rather than assumed.

Assessment serves the learner

Grades and qualifications can have practical value, and learners may request assessment with clear criteria and explanations. But a mark describes a particular performance, not a person's worth. We value what learners can remember, adapt and use independently months or years later. Time spent on a platform is not proof of learning. No compulsory attendance counters, rankings, streaks or guilt for taking a break.

Independence, not dependence

Lifelong learning is an opportunity, not an obligation. Learners may leave and return whenever they wish. Technology should support teachers and human relationships, not replace them or demand attention. We aim for accessible, privacy-conscious tools, responsible use of computing resources, fair commercial practices and fair taxation. We reject manipulative retention and the sale of learner data.

English Unbound

First project · Work in progress

English is the medium. Life is the subject. Curiosity is the driving force.

Meet Pip, a little seed and learning companion. Explore everyday encounters, nature, cultures, creative projects, guided language lessons and a personal workbook. Learn with purpose. Communicate with confidence. Pip is currently a scripted companion; optional natural conversation is a future ambition, not a feature available today.

The prototype is being prepared for private adult learner testing as a Windows application. It is not yet a public download, a validated language assessment or a complete course. Feedback will help shape its development. We intend to keep the core learning experience independent of any particular AI provider.
